Curriculum

Week-by-week course syllabus

A practical, project-based curriculum: two months of UI/UX design and Figma, then a month of basic HTML, responsive design and your portfolio.

Month 1

Design foundations

Week 1Introduction to UI/UX & design thinking

What UI/UX design is, the design process and how real product teams work.

Week 2Design principles & typography

Hierarchy, alignment, contrast, fonts and readable layouts.

Week 3Colour, spacing & grids

Colour theory, spacing systems and layout grids for clean, consistent designs.

Week 4UX research & user flows

User personas, journey maps, information architecture and user flows.

Month 2

Figma & UI/UX design

Week 5Figma essentials

Frames, auto-layout, styles and building your first screens in Figma.

Week 6Wireframes & prototypes

Low and high-fidelity wireframes and clickable prototypes.

Week 7Components & design systems

Reusable components, variants and a mini design system.

Week 8Website & mobile app UI

Designing complete landing pages and mobile app screens.

Month 3

HTML, responsive & portfolio

Week 9Basic HTML5

Page structure, semantic tags, text, images, links and forms.

Week 10CSS3 basics

Styling with CSS — colours, fonts, the box model, Flexbox and Grid basics.

Week 11Responsive design

Media queries and mobile-first layouts — turn a Figma design into a responsive web page.

Week 12Live project, portfolio & certificate

A real design project, portfolio case studies, interview prep and your course certificate.

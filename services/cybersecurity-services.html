Why Act Early

Small gaps cause the biggest problems

Many incidents begin with something ordinary: a reused password, a missed update, or an exposed admin page.

A regular security review finds these gaps early, when fixing them is far cheaper than recovering from a breach.

What is a penetration test?

A penetration test is an authorized, simulated attack on your systems. It shows which weaknesses could really be exploited, so you can fix them.

How often should I run a security audit?

Many businesses review security at least once a year and after major changes, such as a new product release or infrastructure move.

Will testing affect my live website?

We agree timing and limits in advance, and can test on a staging copy to reduce any risk to live users.

Can you guarantee my system cannot be hacked?

No honest provider can promise that. Our work reduces your risk and helps your team respond faster if something happens.

Do you help with ISO 27001 or GDPR?

We can help you find technical gaps and prepare. Formal certification comes from an accredited body, and legal questions need a qualified adviser.

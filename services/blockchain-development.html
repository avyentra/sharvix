What is a smart contract?

A smart contract is a program stored on a blockchain that runs automatically when set conditions are met, such as releasing tokens on a schedule.

Which blockchains do you work with?

We commonly work with EVM-compatible networks such as Ethereum, BNB Chain, and Polygon. We recommend a chain based on your costs, users, and goals.

Do my smart contracts need an audit?

If contracts will hold real funds, we recommend an independent security audit before launch, in addition to our own testing.

Can you design tokenomics?

We can help structure supply, allocation, and vesting for your product. Legal and financial advice should come from qualified professionals.

Do you offer legal advice for token launches?

No. Crypto rules differ by country, so please consult a lawyer about your token and the markets you plan to serve.

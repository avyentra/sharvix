Native Or Cross-Platform

Choosing the right way to build your app

Native apps give the deepest device access and top performance. Cross-platform apps reach both stores from one codebase, which often suits a first release.

We compare both for your features, budget, and timeline, then recommend one clearly.

Native app

  • Deepest device access
  • Top performance
  • Built for one platform at a time

Cross-platform app

  • One codebase for iOS and Android
  • Often suits a first release
  • Helps control cost and time

Mobile app questions

How much does it cost to build a mobile app?

Cost depends on screens, features, and whether you need a backend. We share a clear estimate after understanding your idea.

How long does app development take?

A focused first version often takes a few months. Complex apps take longer, and store review adds a little extra time.

Should I build for iOS or Android first?

It depends on where your customers are. If you need both, a cross-platform build can reach them with one codebase.

Will you publish the app for me?

Yes. We prepare and submit it through your developer accounts and help respond to any store review feedback.

What happens after launch?

We can maintain the app, watch for crashes, support new OS versions, and add features as you learn from real users.

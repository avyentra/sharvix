Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

1. Introduction

Sharvix Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, contact us or enrol in our training program, and how we use and protect it.

2. Information we collect

We only collect information that you choose to share with us, such as:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and city
  • Details about your project, budget or training preferences
  • Any message you send us through forms, email or WhatsApp

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also record basic technical data such as your browser type and pages visited.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and share quotes or proposals
  • To process training enrolments and contact you about your batch
  • To send updates you have asked for (you can opt out at any time)
  • To improve our website and services

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

4. Forms and third-party services

Our website forms are delivered to our inbox using a third-party form service (FormSubmit). Messages sent via WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. These services may process limited technical data to provide their service.

5. Cookies

Our website does not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

6. Data security & retention

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and keep it only for as long as needed to respond to you, deliver our services or meet legal requirements.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Just email us and we will respond as soon as possible.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated date.

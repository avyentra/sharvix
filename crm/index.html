Data & Approval Controls

Google Sheet: only your signed-in Google account can read these records. The browser uses read-only access.

Refresh interval: every 60 seconds while the tab is open, after connection. Saved CRM changes appear after the next successful refresh.

Email rule: ChatGPT never sends new outreach, follow-ups or replies without your explicit approval of that individual message.

CRM automation: after an approved Gmail send or shortlist, the ChatGPT workflow must update Google Sheets. The page cannot independently intercept ChatGPT or Gmail activity.

Public repository: no buyer contact details, Gmail thread IDs, private budgets or access tokens.
